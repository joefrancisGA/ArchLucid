## What not to claim

- Do **not** invent **dollar savings**, **percentage waste**, or **optimized bill** outcomes.
- Do **not** present the review as a substitute for **AWS Cost Explorer**, **FinOps dashboards**, or **internal chargeback** systems.
- Do **not** attribute **SCP compliance** of a live organization to this exercise.

Numbers, SKUs, and account names are **placeholders**. Cost and policy **posture** must be validated against your real AWS estate and organization design.
