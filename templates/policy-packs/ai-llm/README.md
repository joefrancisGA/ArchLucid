# AI / LLM policy pack (template)

`policy-pack.json` in this folder is a **starter** vertical pack for **generative AI and LLM** workloads on Azure: inference, RAG, tool-calling, and governance hooks.

- Do not treat this pack as a **responsible-AI certification**, **model safety guarantee**, or **legal clearance** for production AI.
- Pair with buyer-safe demo disclaimers; no real customer prompts or confidential corpora.

**Related templates:** [`../saas/`](../saas/), [`../healthcare/`](../healthcare/) (if PHI-adjacent RAG applies).
