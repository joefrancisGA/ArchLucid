# Billing and plans

Manage your ArchLucid subscription, payment method, invoices, seats, and usage from **Billing and plans** in workspace settings.

## How billing works {#how-billing-works}

- **Trial** — New workspaces can start on a trial. When the trial ends, choose a paid plan to continue without interruption.
- **Subscription** — Paid plans activate after checkout. Renewal follows your selected plan on the billing cycle shown in **Billing and plans**.
- **Seats** — Plans include a set number of workspace seats. Add seats from **Billing and plans** when your team grows.
- **AI usage** — Monthly AI usage is tracked in **Billing and plans** and **AI usage** settings. Prepaid credits can supplement included allowance on paid plans.
- **Changes and cancellation** — Workspace administrators can update payment details, change plans, or cancel from **Billing and plans**.
