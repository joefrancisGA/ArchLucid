# Governance approval

Learn how architecture work moves from submission to approval, revision, or rejection.

Governance approval connects a finalized architecture package to an auditable decision. Solution architects submit work for review, governance approvers record decisions, and supporting evidence stays linked for diligence and audit.

## Governance workflow {#governance-workflow}

1. Prepare architecture package
2. Resolve required findings
3. Submit for approval
4. Review evidence and risk
5. Approve, reject, or request revision through comments
6. Record and audit the decision

## Role guides {#role-guides}

Solution architects finalize packages and submit approval requests. Governance approvers inspect evidence and record decisions. Security and procurement reviewers use trust materials and linked evidence. Platform engineers attach CI, deployment, and operational validation.

## Statuses {#statuses}

Draft, Submitted, Under review, Approved, Rejected, and Superseded are explained in customer language on the in-app help page. Rejection with reviewer comments is how revision is requested before resubmission.

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

Finalize the architecture package, resolve blocking findings, attach evidence, and confirm submission permission before sending an approval request.

## Decision outcomes {#decision-outcomes}

Approved requests may proceed to environment release and activation. Rejected requests retain audit history and may be resubmitted after revision.

## Common actions {#common-actions}

Use the governance workflow, dashboard, risk register, audit log, and trust center from the in-app action links.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

If submission or approval controls are unavailable, verify permissions, review finalize state, and reload the workflow for the selected review.
