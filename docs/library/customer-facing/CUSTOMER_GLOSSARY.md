# Customer glossary

The in-app glossary at `/help/glossary` is the canonical customer-facing terminology reference.

**Architecture package** is the buyer-facing noun for the durable review record (findings, decisions, evidence, signed review record, and exports). Prefer that term over the legacy *review package*.

Use `/help/glossary` for definitions of review, evidence, governance, and organization terms encountered in ArchLucid.

Engineering field taxonomies and record schemas are documented separately for contributors only.
