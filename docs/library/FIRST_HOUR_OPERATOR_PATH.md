# First-review guide

ArchLucid is designed to help architects move from architecture evidence to a signed, reviewable record. For your first session, focus on completing one architecture review. You can add cloud connections, governance workflows, integrations, and reporting later.

## Pilot first. Operate later.

Your first goal is not to configure the whole platform. Your first goal is to prove that ArchLucid can take architecture evidence, produce useful findings, link those findings to evidence, record decisions, and generate an architecture review you can share.

## Four-step first-review path

| Step | What you do | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Start a new architecture review** | Submit a brief, diagram, IaC file, design note, or evidence-only ZIP. | An architecture review is created and appears in your workspace. |
| **2. Review findings** | Inspect the findings, assumptions, severity, rationale, and evidence links. | Important findings are visible and understandable. |
| **3. Finalize the architecture review** | Record decisions, confirm evidence, and finalize the signed review record. | The architecture review has a signed review record and audit trail. |
| **4. Review artifacts** | Download or inspect the outputs before sharing with sponsors, governance boards, security reviewers, procurement, or audit stakeholders. | The proof packet or review artifact is ready to share. |

## What can wait until later

Do not spend the first session configuring every advanced feature. After the first review is complete, you can connect cloud evidence, invite reviewers, configure policy packs, open governance workflows, and tune reporting.

- Cloud connections can wait unless they are needed for the first review.
- Governance workflows can wait until an architecture review exists.
- Reporting becomes more useful after one or more reviews are finalized.
- Integrations can be added after the core review path is proven.

## What good looks like

- The architecture review has clear findings.
- Important findings trace to evidence.
- Missing evidence is identified instead of guessed.
- Decisions are recorded.
- Risks or exceptions are created only when needed.
- The signed review record is understandable.
- The proof packet can be shared without embarrassment.

## Recommended first session

- Start with one real architecture artifact or run the sample review.
- Review the top findings.
- Confirm or add supporting evidence.
- Record at least one decision.
- Finalize the review.
- Open the generated artifacts.
