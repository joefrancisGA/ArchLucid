# 2. Executive Summary

### (A) Overall Headline Readiness
ArchLucid is a highly structured, enterprise-grade architecture review platform. It scores **88.16%** on the requested quality model (up from 82.88% after engineering improvements **Imp-2–4** and **Imp-6–7**; **Imp-1** and **Imp-5** remain owner/V1.1 deferred). The product is capable of taking an architecture request, running it through a multi-agent pipeline (Topology, Cost, Compliance, Critic), and producing a versioned golden manifest with evidence-linked findings. It has strong engineering foundations (Dapper, DbUp, OpenAPI snapshotting) and enterprise features (OIDC/SAML/SCIM, RBAC, RLS, audit logs).

### (B) Procurement / Market Motion Realism
Procurement friction will be high for broad enterprise rollouts due to the lack of a CPA-issued SOC 2 report, third-party pen-test publication, and live commerce un-hold. However, these are explicitly deferred to V1.1/V2 and do not penalize headline readiness. The trust posture is honest (Trust Center, self-assessment, least-privilege Azure access).

### Commercial Picture
The commercial motion is currently sales-led and service-led. The product has strong ROI calculation mechanics (tenant-adjusted savings, disposition-aware basis) and sponsor-ready exports (board packs, value reports). The main blocker is the lack of real customer proof packets and published references.

### Enterprise Picture
Enterprise architects will appreciate the per-tenant database isolation, policy packs, governance workflows, and Azure-native deployment. Adoption is constrained by the density of the operator UI and the deferral of first-party ITSM/chat connectors (Jira, ServiceNow, Teams) to V1.1.

### Engineering Picture
The engineering base is deep and rigorous, with 95% merged-line coverage, mutation testing, and deterministic orchestration. The main risks are the mid-strangle architecture (dual authority/coordinator semantics) and the fact that real-mode quad-agent end-to-end output quality remains owner-ops-dependent.
