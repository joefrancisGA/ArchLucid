## Executive Summary

### (A) Overall Headline Readiness

ArchLucid is a real, broad V1-shaped product, not a slideware prototype. The strongest evidence is the request -> execute -> commit -> manifest/artifact path, SQL persistence, operator UI, CLI, OpenAPI discipline, tenant-aware packaging, audit/event coverage, live E2E gates, observability, and detailed V1 scope control. The weak center is not feature volume. It is whether enterprise users can trust AI-generated findings, confidence, ROI, and governance conclusions without a founder or senior engineer interpreting the system for them.

Weighted readiness is **75.19%** (+0.31 pt after Batch F; +0.62 pt after Batch E; +0.62 pt after Batch D; +1.23 pt after Batch C; +0.89 pt after Batch B). That is credible for guided pilots and early sales-led V1 use. Batch F hardens procurement pack quality evidence, adds quote-request acknowledge/close follow-up on top of aging triage, and locks curated Pilot/Operate tier fixtures to the route registry and operator nav. It is not yet a low-touch enterprise purchase motion.

### (B) Procurement / Market-Motion Realism

Procurement friction remains material even though it is excluded from `(A)`: no CPA SOC 2 report, no third-party pen-test summary, sales-led checkout, and no public reference customer will slow larger buyers. The repo is honest about this through self-assessment, Trust Center posture, DPA/SIG/CAIQ material, and explicit deferrals. That honesty helps, but it does not erase RFP friction.

### Commercial Picture

The commercial story is strong in concept: manual architecture review is expensive, slow, inconsistent, and governance-heavy. ArchLucid has pricing, ROI math, buyer-job walkthroughs, pilot scorecards, and sponsor-facing artifacts. The commercial gap is proof conversion: ROI is still partly estimated, not sufficiently realized from closed-loop remediation/disposition data, and the trial-to-paid motion remains too service-led.

### Enterprise Picture

The enterprise posture is unusually mature for this stage: tenant isolation strategy, audit events, SCIM, OIDC/SAML surfaces, procurement pack generation, policy packs, governance, and operational docs exist. Batch C closed the P0 query-layer scope predicates, scope-to-identity binding, and production secret-source validation called out in the backlog; remaining blockers are trust calibration, customer self-sufficiency, workflow embeddedness, and cross-tenant integration test coverage.

### Engineering Picture

Engineering depth is high, with many tests, Dapper/SQL discipline, OpenAPI snapshotting, live UI/API E2E, metrics, tracing, and modular projects. The risk is complexity. Batches A–D closed PilotStrict/grounding/forensics, disposition-aware ROI and sponsor packets, tenant-scope enforcement, findings backfill uniqueness, and a first DDL/migration-safety slice (system consolidated DDL, MigrateVerify drift sentinels, rolling-deploy lint, release telemetry evidence, post-commit k6); deferred seams remain around full provenance linkage (TB-036), migration catalog backfill (TB-067), and compatibility stubs. The system is maintainable by its authors; it is not yet easy to reason about for a new team.
