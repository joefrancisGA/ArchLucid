## 2. Executive Summary

### (A) Overall Headline Readiness

ArchLucid is credible for guided V1 pilots. It has a coherent Core Pilot path, durable auditability, tenant isolation, API contract discipline, agent quality gates, RAG/eval scaffolding, proof bundles, and sponsor artifacts. The readiness drag is not missing ambition; it is the combination of AI correctness proof, setup burden, and a wide operator surface.

### (B) Procurement / Market-Motion Realism

SOC 2 CPA attestation, third-party pen-test publication, live Marketplace/Stripe un-hold, public references, signed design partner, MCP, V1.1 connectors, AWS/GCP target analysis, and multi-region active/active are deferred or informational and are not deducted from `(A)`. They remain real buyer-friction risks for larger enterprise purchases.

### Commercial Picture

The commercial narrative is strong for founder-led sales: pricing, guided pilot, scorecard, quote-to-pilot pack, sponsor brief, conversion checklist, and evidence bundle exist. Revenue proof still depends on real buyer baselines, credible quality-gate evidence, and a sponsor packet that clearly says send, hold, or defer.

### Enterprise Picture

The enterprise posture is serious: OIDC/SAML, SCIM, database-per-tenant isolation, durable audit, route/tier/policy/nav matrix, procurement pack, trust center, DPA/CAIQ/SIG materials, and production-like config linting. The issue is operational complexity, not lack of enterprise controls.

### Engineering Picture

Engineering depth is high: .NET/Dapper/SQL, DbUp migrations, Next.js UI, OpenAPI snapshots, live UI/API tests, k6, quality gates, RAG evals, data-consistency probes, and OpenTelemetry. The main engineering risk is coordination complexity across modes, gates, scripts, docs, and proof artifacts.

### Deferred Scope Uncertainty

No deferred-scope uncertainty was found. The deferred-scope material was located in `docs/library/V1_SCOPE.md`, `docs/library/V1_DEFERRED.md`, `docs/go-to-market/TRUST_CENTER.md`, `docs/security/SOC2_SELF_ASSESSMENT_2026.md`, and `docs/go-to-market/SOC2_ROADMAP.md`.

2. Retrieval grounding can become stale or fail open.
3. Authority/legacy lifecycle semantics can confuse clients.
4. Configuration complexity can create security and reliability errors.
5. Single-region V1 topology limits availability proof for top-tier buyers.
6. Large generated matrices and docs can drift unless continuously enforced.

## 8. Most Important Truth

ArchLucid is strong enough for guided, evidence-backed architecture review pilots, but not yet simple or self-proving enough to sell as a low-friction enterprise platform without hands-on operator discipline.
