# SecureNow pilot decision path

> **INTERNAL GUIDE.** This is a navigation aid for a controlled evaluation. It does not authorize commerce, imply beta GO, or replace the evidence and owner gates.

## One path from interest to disposition

| Step | Decision question | SecureNow surface | Supporting record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Qualify | Is the use case bounded and authorized? | Home / reviewer materials | [`QUOTE_TO_PILOT_CHECKLIST.md`](QUOTE_TO_PILOT_CHECKLIST.md) §1 |
| 2. Scope | What tenant, subscriptions, retention and review question are in scope? | Environment context and collection setup | [`CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md`](CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md) stage 0 |
| 3. Review | Which possible path matters and why? | Priorities & waves, Path inspect | [`FIXTURE_STORYBOARD.md`](FIXTURE_STORYBOARD.md) |
| 4. Decide change | What intervention, if any, will the customer authorize? | Advisory cut point and remediation context | Customer-owned change record |
| 5. Verify | What does a later capture support? | Verification and evidence lineage | Executive packet; otherwise **NOT VERIFIED** |
| 6. Close | Proceed, extend, or stop? | Sponsor readout | [`PILOT_DECISION_RECORD.md`](PILOT_DECISION_RECORD.md) |

## Required close inputs

Before selecting an outcome, record the sponsor, decision owner, evidence reviewed, limitations, objections and follow-up owner. Preserve partial collection, rejected paths and unresolved analysis gaps.

## Outcome meanings

- **Proceed to commercial discussion:** the sponsor elects to discuss a separately approved scope; it is not a contract or paid-customer claim.
- **Extend with bounded scope:** a named question remains open and has a new scope and review condition.
- **Stop:** the evaluation ends with its evidence and rationale preserved.

SecureNow does not change Azure, certify compliance, prove exploit activity, guarantee savings or infer a clean outcome from missing evidence. Fees stay in the private quote workflow.
