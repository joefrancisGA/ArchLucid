# Scenario pack — later-capture-not-verified

## Intent

Demonstrate honest verification failure when a later capture or attestation element is missing.

## Open

[Storyboard beat 5 — Verify or fail honestly](../FIXTURE_STORYBOARD.md#beat-5--verify-or-fail-honestly)

## Do not say
