# SecureNow quote-to-pilot checklist

Use this sequence to move from a qualified conversation to an owner-approved controlled evaluation without inventing a fee, entitlement, customer result, or release status.

## 1. Qualify before quoting

- [ ] Name the sponsor, technical operator, decision owner, and support contact.
- [ ] Confirm written authority for the tenant, subscriptions, evidence upload, and confidential identifiers.
- [ ] Confirm the buyer wants evidence-linked path prioritization, not autonomous Azure changes, exploit confirmation, a certification, or promised savings.
- [ ] Confirm the proposed scope is Azure-supported and has a defined review question and definition of done.
- [ ] Stop if tenant isolation, data handling, or external change ownership is unresolved.

## 2. Assemble the buyer packet

Send or review these in order:

1. [`QUOTE_COVER.md`](QUOTE_COVER.md) for SKU identity and exclusions.
2. [`CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md`](CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md) for the bounded evaluation and acceptance criteria.
3. [`BUYER_SECURITY_ONE_PAGER.md`](BUYER_SECURITY_ONE_PAGER.md) and the relevant procurement index entries.
4. [`DATA_PROCESSING_SCHEDULE.md`](DATA_PROCESSING_SCHEDULE.md) for negotiation, not as an executed DPA.
5. [`SUPPORT_COMMITMENT.md`](SUPPORT_COMMITMENT.md) with its founder-target and non-SLA label.
6. [`ORDER_FORM_TEMPLATE.md`](ORDER_FORM_TEMPLATE.md) after scope is sufficiently specific.
7. [`PILOT_MEASUREMENT_LOG.md`](PILOT_MEASUREMENT_LOG.md) and [`ROI_COST_INPUT.md`](ROI_COST_INPUT.md) as blank instruments, never as savings evidence.

## 3. Handle pricing safely

- [ ] Use only the owner-approved [`PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md`](PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md) for fee figures, meters, currency, taxes, and evaluation-credit treatment.
- [ ] Keep fee figures out of the quote cover, procurement index, ROI notes, and other buyer-safe template pages.
- [ ] Confirm the selected SKU and its meter in `ORDER_FORM_TEMPLATE.md`.
- [ ] Confirm whether the proposed scope needs an additional subscription or capture bundle.
- [ ] Do not describe reserved offer or plan identifiers as registered Partner Center records.
- [ ] Do not reuse an ArchLucid subscription as a SecureNow entitlement.

## 4. Clear release and legal gates

- [ ] Owner has reviewed pricing, contract, buyer communication, and support terms.
- [ ] Legal has reviewed the data-processing schedule and any customer-specific terms.
- [ ] The beta evidence matrix, live identity checks, foreign-tenant denial, rollback, and support path are recorded as required.
- [ ] The customer understands that SecureNow does not make Azure changes.
- [ ] If any blocking gate is open, record **NO_GO** and use demo-only materials.

## 5. Start and close the pilot

- [ ] Record the approved scope, tenant, retention, operator, escalation route, and start condition.
- [ ] Capture the baseline and pilot timing rows without inventing minutes or savings.
- [ ] Preserve partial collection, uncertainty, rejected paths, and `NOT VERIFIED` outcomes.
- [ ] Obtain a customer-owned change record before discussing a later verification.
- [ ] At close, choose: proceed to commercial discussion, extend with bounded scope, or stop.
- [ ] Do not call a pilot a paid customer, reference customer, ROI result, certification, or successful remediation without the required evidence and owner approval.

## Stop conditions

Stop and escalate to the owner when pricing is not approved, a customer requests a public price, a buyer expects live billing or Marketplace checkout, a contract or DPA is assumed to be executed, or beta remains **NO_GO**. This checklist cannot authorize commerce.
