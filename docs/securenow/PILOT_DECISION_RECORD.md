## Guardrails

- A selected outcome is a human disposition, not an automated product decision.
- **Proceed** does not mean a contract, paid customer, beta GO, verified remediation or ROI result exists.
- **Extend** must name a bounded scope and a review question.
- **Stop** preserves the evidence and reasons; it is not a product failure classification.
