# SecureNow ROI cost input

**C** is the annual product fee plus the customer’s operating cost. The fee is an input. It is not evidence that money was saved.

| Part of C | Where it comes from | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product fee | Owner-approved private quote in [`PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md`](PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md); SKU identifiers **SN-EVAL** and **SN-ANNUAL** | Owner-approved private quote |
| Customer operating cost | The sponsor’s own hosting, staff, and change-control cost for the year | **NOT COLLECTED** |

Do not add these parts until both are known. An unknown operating cost means **C** is not ready.

## What this card does not do

This card does not copy fee amounts from the private quote. It does not fill [`EXECUTIVE_PROOF_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md`](EXECUTIVE_PROOF_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md). It does not claim ROI.
