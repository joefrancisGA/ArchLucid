# SecureNow investigation-effort send rule

A person may share a quantified effort figure only when every gate below is true. Otherwise the figure stays internal.

1. **B** and **A** were written by a person from comparable tasks. The tasks share the same question, the same Azure scope, and the same definition of done. Minutes were divided by 60 only after the minutes existed.
2. **N** is the sponsor’s count of those investigations for a year. **N** is not a path count.
3. **R** is the sponsor’s fully loaded hourly rate. SecureNow does not supply a default rate.
4. **C** includes the owner-approved private quote and a collected customer operating cost. When operating cost is **NOT COLLECTED**, or the sponsor declined to give it, **C** is not ready and the figure stays internal.
5. [`ROI_FIXTURE_WORKSHEET.md`](ROI_FIXTURE_WORKSHEET.md) is not the source. That worksheet stays labeled **FIXTURE**.
6. The sponsor approved the figure for this audience.
7. [`EXECUTIVE_PROOF_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md`](EXECUTIVE_PROOF_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md) stays **ROI not calculated** until a person fills a private copy from these inputs. This rule does not fill the packet.

Do not convert a removed path, a finding count, or an avoided breach into money. Do not copy a fee amount out of [`PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md`](PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md). Do not reuse a percent from [`../go-to-market/ROI_MODEL.md`](../go-to-market/ROI_MODEL.md).
