# SecureNow support commitment

> **OWNER RECORDED SUPPORT COMMITMENT** (2026-10-29). **NOT A CONTRACTUAL SLA.** Not service credits, not a 24×7 NOC, not a hosted availability percentage, and not approval to send this language to a buyer. SecureNow beta remains **NO_GO**. Live billing stays off.

This commitment clones the founder-operated coverage model and the V1 pilot operating model in [ArchLucid support policy](../go-to-market/SUPPORT_POLICY.md). It does not change ArchLucid entitlements, and an ArchLucid Team, Professional, or Enterprise payment does not include this channel.

The private quote sheet’s “business-hours email” means the channel below. Fees stay in [PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md](PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md). Each customer still needs the open gates in [CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md](CONTROLLED_PILOT_OFFER.md), including the unchecked support-commitment line on the owner checklist.

## Who responds

**Joseph Francis** is the first responder. He also holds a separate full-time role. That role permits an initial acknowledgement of **about one hour** during the support hours below.

Additional support staff are hired as revenue permits. Until then there is no second responder and no overnight rotation.

## What buyers get in this phase

Support is **white-glove and founder-led** for an approved evaluation or annual term. The founder is the primary contact for onboarding, collection questions, and issue resolution. Issues do not sit in a tiered support queue. Response is faster than a typical SaaS portal and depends on this one person’s availability.

This phase does not scale to many simultaneous customers. Hired staff are how it scales, and only as revenue permits.

## What this commitment is not

- Not a 24×7 NOC or a staffed-hours promise.
- Not a contractual SLA, and not service credits, unless a later order form says so and is executed.
- Not an uptime percentage.
- Not a data-processing agreement. That agreement is still not approved.
- Not beta release, a Partner Center plan, or permission to invoice.

## Severity

Use **P1** only when one of these is true:

1. Agreed collection or analysis is blocked: no successful snapshot can complete for the authorized scope.
2. The buyer is blocked from the evidence-classified paths or the verification result the evaluation was scoped to produce, and a decision deadline in that scope is active.
3. Suspected tenant data exposure, cross-tenant read, or another security incident.

| Priority | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **P1 — Critical** | One of the three cases above. |
| **P2 — High** | Snapshots complete, but selected paths are missing or mislabeled, or a later snapshot cannot be reported as verified, not verified, or insufficient evidence. |
| **P3 — Medium** | Non-blocking. A workaround exists, or the gap is documentation or cosmetic. |
| **P4 — Low** | Enhancement request or informational question. |
