# SecureNow SKU and meter card

This card names the owner-approved SKU identifiers and their operational meters. Fee figures, currency, tax posture, and credit treatment live only in [`PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md`](PRIVATE_PRICE_SHEET.md).

| SKU | Offer shape | Meter summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SN-EVAL** | Controlled evaluation | 45-day evaluation; up to 2 Azure subscriptions and 4 successful snapshots |
| **SN-ANNUAL** | Annual subscription evidence | One-year term; 3 Azure subscriptions, 24 successful snapshots, and 5 named operators |
| **SN-SUB-ADD** | Annual scope expansion | One additional subscription on an annual term; the private quote sheet cap applies |
| **SN-SNAP-4** | Capture expansion | 4 successful snapshots; unused balance expires at the end of the applicable annual term |

## Boundary notes

- This card does not state a fee, tax amount, discount, or public list price.
- These identifiers are not Partner Center registrations, Stripe prices, or active entitlements.
- An ArchLucid Team, Professional, or Enterprise subscription does not grant SecureNow access.
- Reader-only collection or approved upload is the evaluation boundary; SecureNow does not perform Azure writes.
- Support acknowledgement targets are described in [`SUPPORT_COMMITMENT.md`](SUPPORT_COMMITMENT.md) and are not a contractual SLA.
- The controlled beta remains **NO_GO** until the owner clears the release gates.
