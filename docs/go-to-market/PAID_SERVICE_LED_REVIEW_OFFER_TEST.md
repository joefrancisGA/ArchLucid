## One-page private offer brief

**Offer name:** ArchLucid AI & Cloud Architecture Readiness Review  
**Format:** Fixed-scope paid engagement (2–3 weeks calendar; 1 architecture domain)  
**Price band (private quote):** **$5,000–$10,000 USD** standard scope — lightweight **$1,500–$3,000** only when brief is narrowly bounded  
**Delivery:** Founder-led or named architect + ArchLucid as **delivery infrastructure**

**Buyer gets:**

- Executive summary and architecture evidence inventory aligned to the submitted packet
- Risk register and recommended actions with evidence-basis labels
- Final **Architecture Review Report** (DOCX/PDF; whitelabel available)
- Finalized ArchLucid architecture package with audit trail (tenant scoped)

**Buyer does not get (unless separately contracted):**

- SOC 2 CPA attestation or third-party pen-test report
- V1.1 connectors (Jira, ServiceNow, Slack, Teams, Confluence)
- Marketplace self-serve checkout or MCP ecosystem
- Guaranteed ROI dollars without buyer-provided baselines

---

## Qualification questions (first call)

1. What decision must this review support in the next **30–60 days** (ARB, funding, migration go/no-go)?
2. What architecture packet can you provide in the first week (brief, diagrams, IaC excerpt)?
3. Who signs off on findings — and who needs the sponsor export?
4. Is live Azure OpenAI execution required, or is labeled simulator walkthrough acceptable for phase 1?
5. What baseline can you supply for review-cycle hours and prep effort (even rough)?
6. Any deferred requirements we should label explicitly (CPA SOC 2, pen test, specific connectors)?

**Disqualifiers (polite defer):**

- Buyer wants only a chatbot replacement with no governance record
- No sponsor or decision owner — "research only" with no next step
- Requires V1.1 connector automation as go-live blocker before any review value
