# Record vs Practice on the Working desk

Use the Working desk chooser to pick the review type that matches your intent.

## Record

Record is the sealed-record path. Choose Record when you are building evidence that must hold up for finalize, export, and sponsor review. Real execution mode is required for record-complete artifacts.

## Practice

Practice is for dry-runs. Choose Practice when you are learning the flow, rehearsing objections, or validating UI behavior without claiming sponsor proof. Simulator output is labeled practice and is not sponsor proof.

## Simulator is not sponsor proof

Simulator and fallback execution modes keep deterministic or substituted output honest. They do not satisfy record-complete gates or sponsor-grade claims unless explicitly labeled as practice.

## Legacy terms

Help search and stored preferences may still reference **Career** and **Rehearsal** as aliases for Record and Practice (ADR 0097). Slug `career-rehearsal-doors` is unchanged.
