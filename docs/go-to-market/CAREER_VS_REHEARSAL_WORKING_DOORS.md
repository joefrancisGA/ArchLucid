# Career vs Rehearsal on the Working desk

Use the Working desk chooser to pick the door that matches your intent.

## Career

Career is the sealed-record path. Choose Career when you are building evidence that must hold up for finalize, export, and sponsor review. Real execution mode is required for career-complete artifacts.

## Rehearsal

Rehearsal is practice. Choose Rehearsal when you are learning the flow, rehearsing objections, or validating UI behavior without claiming sponsor proof. Simulator output is labeled rehearsal and is not sponsor proof.

## Simulator is not sponsor proof

Simulator and fallback execution modes keep deterministic or substituted output honest. They do not satisfy career-complete gates or sponsor-grade claims unless explicitly labeled as rehearsal practice.
