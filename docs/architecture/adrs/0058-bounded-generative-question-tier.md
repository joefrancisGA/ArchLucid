# ADR 0058: Bounded generative question tier (L2g) + retrospective question mining

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-07-12
**Deciders:** Owner + Architecture review
**Amends:** [ADR 0051](0051-question-selection-engine.md) (question selection engine)
**Related:** [ADR 0031](0031-cross-tenant-pattern-library.md) (cross-tenant wall), [ADR 0048](0048-socratic-intake-mutable-draft-lifecycle.md) (draft lifecycle), [ADR 0050](0050-feasibility-classification-transparency-trail.md) (transparency trail), `docs/ARCHLUCID_FOUNDATIONAL_DESIGN_DEBATE.md` (R7–R10), `docs/architecture/ai_initiative_governance.md` (AI initiative readiness wedge)

## Context

ADR 0051 demoted the LLM from question **generator** to **selector over a bounded, curated corpus** and barred any L2 implementation "before golden-cohort calibration data exists." Both rulings rested on one claim: free-generated questions are **ungradeable** — there is no ground truth for "was that the right question?"

Two things have changed:

1. **A bootstrapping flaw in the original flywheel.** L3 (the learning loop) promotes useful questions into packs — but a pure L0/L1 system never *produces* novel candidates, and the golden-cohort data that would unlock L2 is itself generated by asking questions. The precondition starves the mechanism it gates. The flywheel cannot start.
2. **The AI initiative readiness wedge** (see `ai_initiative_governance.md`) targets a domain — AI/GenAI/agentic proposals — that reinvents itself faster than pack authoring cycles. A deterministic-only question surface in this domain goes stale in months, and staleness is a competitive failure mode ("adapt or die" — owner ruling, 2026-07-12). The owner further ruled that the question corpus and its grading data are a **primary platform IP asset**, worth deliberate, platform-funded investment.

The owner's key insight: **behavioral signals substitute for the missing ground truth.** Skips, junk/joke answers, architect edits and suppressions, and (retrospectively) whether a gap surfaced later that an unasked question would have caught — these grade a generated question without a pre-existing golden cohort. Grading by observation replaces grading by oracle.

This ADR therefore amends ADR 0051 in two narrow places and leaves the rest intact.

## Decision

### A. Live bounded generative tier (L2g)

1. **When:** only after L0/L1 are exhausted and the proposal remains under-determined — for **any** proposal the active packs cannot cover (not template-restricted).
2. **Volume cap:** at most **3** generated questions per draft session by default; at most **5** when the `ai-governance` specialty template is active. Caps are configuration, not code constants.
3. **Never load-bearing.** Generated questions are **exploratory tier only**: they can never be `MUST`, never block the `Submitted` transition (ADR 0048), and never gate a feasibility verdict or readiness disposition. ADR 0051's deterministic termination rule ("done = all MUST answered; the LLM never decides when to stop") survives unchanged.
4. **No fabricated mappings.** A generated question may **not** assert a policy, regulatory, or standards mapping unless the mapping resolves to a real rule key in an assigned pack version. Unmapped questions are visibly labeled **exploratory** in the UI (e.g. "ArchLucid is asking this because your proposal is unusual — it maps to no specific policy yet"). Odd-but-honest is acceptable; confident-and-invented is the prohibited failure class.
5. **Platform-funded confidence gate.** Before display, each candidate passes content safety plus a frontier-model judge scoring **relevance, clarity, and safety**; judge calls are attributed to the **platform** (ArchLucid), not the tenant, via the existing tiered completion router and AI budget policy. Below-threshold candidates are discarded silently — the customer never sees rejects.
6. **Full provenance.** Every displayed generated question records: generating model + prompt version, judge model + scores, and session context hash — so a question's track record travels with it if promoted.
7. **Telemetry:** skip rate, junk-answer detection, architect edits/suppressions are logged per question and flow into k-anonymized aggregates (R10 channel).

### B. Retrospective question mining (nightly batch)

1. **A nightly Worker job** analyzes completed draft sessions and finalized runs and asks, with full hindsight context (answers, findings, dispositions, resubmission outcomes): *what question, asked at intake, would have prevented an observed gap — even if it was never asked?*
2. **Candidates enter a `QuestionProposal` vetting queue** for the internal operator — the same propose → human-vet → promote shape as the existing evidence-proposal pipeline (`AgentCuratedEvidenceProposer` → operator Promote), not a new pattern.
3. **Vetting checks two things:** usefulness **and generalization** — the candidate must be de-tenanted into a generic form before it may enter the platform corpus. Anything tenant-identifiable is rejected or rewritten.
4. **Dedup/cluster with frequency counting.** Identical or near-identical candidates arising from many sessions are clustered; **cross-tenant frequency is the value-of-information signal** ADR 0051's O1-remainder was waiting for.
5. **K-anonymity promotion gate:** a candidate is eligible for the platform corpus only when its triggering pattern is observed across at least *k* tenants (k configurable), operationalizing R10 rather than relying on operator judgment alone.
6. **Promotion follows existing pack discipline unchanged:** human gate (R9), SemVer uplift, no silent row mutation. A promoted question becomes a deterministic L1 question.

### C. What this amends in ADR 0051 — and what it does not

| ADR 0051 clause | Disposition |
|---|---|
| "L2 does **not** free-generate questions" | **Amended** — bounded generation permitted under A.1–A.7 only |
| "No L2/VoI implementation until golden-cohort data exists" | **Amended** — L2g at small N needs a quality **floor** (the judge gate), not a ranking; its telemetry plus the mining pipeline **produce** the calibration data. Full L2 selection/VoI over large candidate sets remains OPEN (O1-remainder) and remains blocked until that data exists |
| L0/L1 layering, packs own questions (R8) | **Unchanged** |
| Deterministic termination (MUST-set gate) | **Unchanged** |
| Human-gated promotion outside runtime (R9) | **Unchanged** — reused as-is by B.6 |
| Cross-tenant wall: k-anonymized aggregates only (R10 / ADR 0031) | **Unchanged and absolute** — raw cross-tenant Q&A reuse stays forbidden; B.3–B.5 exist to enforce it |

## Trade-offs

Bounded generation **gains** adaptability in fast-moving domains, a corpus that compounds into defensible platform IP, and — via mining — the calibration data that unblocks O1-remainder; it **gives up** the original ADR's cleaner promise. "Nondeterminism decreases monotonically" is softened to **"nondeterminism is bounded, graded, and converts into deterministic pack content over time."** We accept that an occasional generated question will be odd or even mildly off-putting; the owner explicitly prefers that risk over platform staleness, and the blast radius in the analyst workflow is one skip (the architect is the only viewer). We **reject** unbounded generation (still the dangerous-oracle pattern), **reject** letting generated questions carry governance weight (they never block or gate), and **reject** shipping mined candidates without human vetting (hallucinated requirements entering the corpus would be a trust-fatal defect). The nightly miner is expected to be the **primary** corpus-growth channel (higher quality per candidate, zero customer-facing risk); live L2g is the secondary channel for in-the-moment novelty.

## Constraints

- **Generated questions are never evidence and never sealed** — they live on the draft (ADR 0048), outside the sealed-evidence boundary (ADR 0039).
- **Judge + generation costs are platform-attributed and budget-capped** via the existing AI budget policy surfaces; a runaway generation loop must trip the cap, not the tenant's bill.
- **Content-safety precheck applies** to generated text exactly as to user free-text at admission (ADR 0048).
- **Single-DDL-file house rule** — any new `QuestionProposal` queue table is added to the unified schema script.
- **The cross-tenant wall is not relaxed by this ADR in any respect.** Anything richer than k-anonymized aggregates is a new ADR with DPA consequences (unchanged from ADR 0051).
- Mining runs are tenant-scoped reads; candidate generalization (B.3) is the boundary crossing point and the vetting queue is its control.

## Expected impact

- **System:** a generation+judge step in the draft question path (behind L0/L1 exhaustion); a nightly Worker job; a `QuestionProposal` queue + operator vetting surface (modeled on evidence proposals).
- **Security posture:** neutral-to-positive — generation is bounded, judged, labeled, and non-load-bearing; the mining pipeline adds a controlled, auditable channel where an uncontrolled one (ad-hoc pack edits from anecdote) might otherwise emerge.
- **Operations:** the internal operator gains a recurring vetting duty; queue depth and promotion rate become health metrics; judge threshold and caps need occasional tuning.
- **Cost:** platform-funded and small — live L2g is capped per session; mining is nightly batch at economy/batch pricing with premium judging only on clustered survivors.
- **Teams:** platform owns generation, judging, mining, and the queue; pack authors consume promoted questions through the existing SemVer process.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** the learning flywheel actually starts; the question corpus becomes a compounding, graded IP asset; the AI-governance wedge keeps pace with a fast-moving domain; O1-remainder gains its data source.
- **Negative:** a new always-on quality duty (judge thresholds, vetting queue) and a softened determinism story that must be explained honestly in trust materials.
- **Follow-ups:** define judge rubric + thresholds; add `QuestionProposal` DDL to the unified schema; implement the nightly miner on the Worker; extend `ElicitationQuestions` with conditional visibility (deterministic L1 branching — separate, unblocked work item); revisit O1-remainder once mining frequency data accumulates; update trust-center language on question generation and labeling.
