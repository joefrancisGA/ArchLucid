# ABQ-16 — Publish honest hunt counters (`effective-bugs` + bugs>hunts lint)

**After ABQ-06 and ABQ-10 (shipped).** Do not retune the speed formula except to **display** the numbers scoring already uses. Do not rewrite historical `bugs-found` integers.

## Goal

Humans reading the ledger and picker preview can see **effective bugs** (`min(bugs-found, hunts)` when `hunts > 0`) next to the raw `bugs-found` count. A lint fails when a **non-retired** zone claims `bugs-found > hunts` (impossible under one-hit-per-hunt). Retired mega-zones may keep inflated historical `bugs-found` with an explicit footnote / allowlist — do not silently rewrite them.

## Why

The ledger still publishes raw totals that look like yield: on the order of **94** zones, **~1472** hunts, **~4557** `bugs-found` (**~3.10 bugs/hunt**). About **40** zones report `bugs-found > hunts`. Worst: retired `archlucid-core` hunts≈377 / bugs≈2802. ABQ-06/11 already stop that inflation from **ranking** the picker (`Get-MeanHuntsPerBug` forfeits speed unless `hunts > 0 && bugs > 0 && bugs <= hunts`). The **published integers** still lie. The validity audit (`docs/library/AL_BUG_HUNT_VALIDITY_AUDIT.md`) already says **57.2% (2321/4061)** of proven rows are treadmill; `archlucid-core` is **82.7%** treadmill, other zones **0%**. Dashboards that cite `bugs-found` without `effective-bugs` re-Goodhart the KPI.
