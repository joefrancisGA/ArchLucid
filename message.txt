refactor: decouple ArchLucid.Core instrumentation from application metrics state

- Moved `FirstTenantFunnelEventNames` to `ArchLucid.Application.Telemetry`.
- Moved `CircuitBreakerGateMetricsRegistry` to `ArchLucid.Host.Core.Diagnostics`.
- Moved `LlmTenantBudgetRemainingGaugeState` and `LlmTenantBudgetUtilizationGaugeState` to `ArchLucid.Host.Core.Hosted`.
- Decoupled `ArchLucidInstrumentation` in Core by replacing hard-coded metric state dependencies with delegate injection points (e.g. `SetCircuitBreakerSnapshotReader`, `SetFirstTenantFunnelEventNameValidator`).
- Configured these delegates at startup within `ObservabilityExtensions` using `LlmTenantBudgetUtilizationMetricsHostedService` gauge states and the newly moved types.
